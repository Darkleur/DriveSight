Uses the current video or image. For driving numbers, pick a dashcam clip with "Video file" first.

FPS = 1000 / avg ms: how many frames per second the model alone could process. Match fp32 = share of the fp32 model's boxes (same label, overlap IoU ≥ 0.5) that this precision also found on the same frames. It measures agreement with full precision, not accuracy: there are no human-made labels here.
